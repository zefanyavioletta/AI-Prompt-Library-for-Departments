## 1. Importance of AI Usage for Company
* **Operations & HR:** Employees deal with long meetings and repetitive document writing. We can use AI to summarize meeting transcripts, draft internal announcements, and turn rough notes into clear policy guidelines.
* **Marketing:** Teams must create large amounts of content quickly. AI is very useful to brainstorm campaign ideas, generate social media captions, and rewrite copy for different audiences.
* **Finance & Supply Chain:** Employees spend hours manually copying data from messy emails, vendor invoices, or PDF reports into Excel. It's highly effective to use AI to extract text data and format it into clean tables.

## 2. Operations & HR Prompts

### Template: Meeting Transcript to Action Items
[ROLE]
You are an executive assistant summarizing key operational decisions.

[TASK]
Convert the provided meeting raw notes into a structured summary for management.

[CONTEXT]
Meeting Topic: [Insert Topic]
Attendees: [Insert Attendees]
Raw Notes:
---
[Paste meeting notes here]
---

[OUTPUT FORMAT]
1. Executive Summary (2-3 sentences)
2. Key Decisions Made (bullet points)
3. Action Items (Table with columns: Task, Owner, Deadline)
4. Unresolved Risks or Blockers
